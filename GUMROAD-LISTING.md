# Gumroad product listing draft

## Product name

micxio — Voice Changer for Windows & Linux

## Short description

Transform recorded audio or your live microphone with private, studio-style
voice effects. Your audio is processed on your own computer.

## Full description

micxio is a desktop voice changer for creators, gamers, streamers, and anyone
who wants to transform recorded audio without uploading it to a cloud service.

Choose from twelve built-in effects including Female, Male, Robot, Child,
Monster, Alien, Radio, and Echo. Create and save your own voices with pitch,
echo, and brightness controls. You can also record directly from a microphone
or send the selected effect to your headphones in Live Voice mode.

### Included

- Voice changer for Windows and Linux
- Twelve built-in voice effects
- Custom voice presets: create, edit, and delete
- Batch conversion to MP3, WAV, or FLAC
- Microphone recording and Live Voice mode
- English, Russian, Simplified Chinese, and Japanese UI
- Local audio processing — no account or cloud upload required

### System requirements

- Windows 10/11 or a recent Linux desktop
- A microphone and headphones for recording or Live Voice mode
- Internet access only when the audio-processing engine needs to be downloaded
  for the first time

### Download contents

Download the ZIP that matches the buyer's operating system, extract it, and
open `micxio` (Linux) or `micxio.exe` (Windows). The application stores custom
voice presets in the buyer's local settings folder.

### Important notes

- Use headphones with Live Voice to avoid feedback.
- The product is for personal use. Redistribution and resale are not allowed;
  see the included LICENSE file.
- Buyers must have the rights to any audio they process.

## Suggested Gumroad setup

1. Create a Digital product and upload separate Windows and Linux ZIP files as
   product versions.
2. Add a License key block if you want activation keys for paid buyers.
3. Paste the short and full descriptions above, then upload
   `assets/micxio-icon.png` as the product cover.
4. Test the buyer download flow with a $0 test version before publishing.
