# micxio quick start

1. Extract the ZIP file completely before opening the app.
2. Open `micxio` on Linux or `micxio.exe` on Windows.
3. Click **Add audio files**, choose an effect, then click **Apply voice effect**.
4. Processed files are saved in the source folder unless you choose another
   output folder.

## Custom voices

Use **Add custom** to create a named voice with your own Pitch, Echo, and
Brightness settings. Select it later to edit or delete it. Your custom voices
are saved locally on your computer.

## Recording and Live Voice

Click **Record** to make a new audio file from your microphone. Click
**Live Voice** to hear an effect immediately through your chosen output.
Use headphones and start with a low volume to prevent feedback.

## Help

The audio-processing engine may download automatically the first time the app
needs it. After that, normal conversion works offline. If the app cannot see a
microphone or output device, check the operating-system permissions and audio
settings, then restart the app.
